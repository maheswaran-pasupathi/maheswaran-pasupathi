# Battery Systems & Performance Simulation Portfolio

**Focus:** connecting electrical demand, heat generation, thermal control and usable vehicle performance from cell to vehicle level.

This portfolio is for battery-system and performance-simulation roles where thermal behaviour is a key determinant of charging, range, power availability and safety. It is rooted in thermal/system simulation and does not claim BMS software ownership, utility-scale BESS deployment or certification responsibility.

## Industrial keywords

Battery systems · battery performance · cell-to-pack modelling · heat generation · SOC–temperature interaction · temperature-dependent limits · charging and fast charging · thermal control · battery cooling · pre-conditioning · usable energy · auxiliary loads · range impact · power derating · thermal propagation · reduced-order model · system simulation · parameter estimation · validation · surrogate modelling · physics-informed AI

## Relevant 1D activities

| Activity | Typical decision |
|---|---|
| Cell/module/pack heat-generation model | How does electrical demand become a thermal load across the operating map? |
| SOC–temperature and power-limit study | How do low/high temperature and charge state constrain usable power or charging? |
| Vehicle energy and range model | What is the range effect of HVAC, pumps, compressor and thermal-control choices? |
| Thermal pre-conditioning strategy | Is the energy spent before a route or charge event justified by performance gain? |
| Reduced-order / surrogate model | Can repeated duty-cycle or optimisation studies run faster while maintaining physical checks? |
| Model calibration | Which test signals identify uncertain thermal, electrical or flow parameters? |

## Relevant 3D activities

| Activity | Typical decision |
|---|---|
| Cell/module/pack thermal model | Which local temperature gradient is hidden by a system-level average? |
| Thermal interfaces and heat paths | Which contact or material path controls cell-to-coolant resistance? |
| Local propagation / safety study | How could heat transfer from one location affect neighbouring cells or modules? |
| Cooling concept comparison | Which local design improves uniformity without unacceptable pressure loss or mass? |
| 3D-to-1D reduction | How should detailed heat-transfer and flow results be converted into system-model parameters? |

## Demonstrated engineering evidence

- Thermal-system modelling connecting battery cooling, HVAC/e-powertrain loads and vehicle duty-cycle behaviour.
- Coupled 1D/3D battery thermal and propagation studies with calorimeter and thermocouple correlation context.
- Engineering automation and scientific-ML exploration for post-processing, knowledge retrieval and surrogate workflows, with physics and validation retained as the governing checks.
