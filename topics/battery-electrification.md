# Battery & Electrification

This page collects the battery and electrification topics I work on and the engineering questions behind them.

## Topics I cover

- Battery thermal management
- Cell/pack temperature distribution
- Thermal runaway and propagation modelling
- 1D–3D coupled thermal workflows
- Electro-thermal busbar and connection heating
- Cooling-system interpretation
- Test/calorimeter correlation
- Thermal safety and design sensitivity

## How I think about the problem

Battery thermal work is not only about predicting a maximum temperature. I look at where heat is generated, how it moves through cells, interfaces and cooling paths, how strongly the result depends on assumptions, and whether the model can reproduce measured behaviour closely enough to support a design decision.

For safety problems such as thermal runaway, model scope and fidelity matter: source representation, propagation path, thermal contact, boundary conditions and coupling strategy can dominate the result.

## Selected evidence

- Battery thermal and thermal-runaway modelling with coupled 3D–1D propagation assessment.
- Correlation of modelled cell-skin temperatures against calorimeter/test data.
- Electro-thermal hotspot prediction used to support geometry/contact-interface decisions.
