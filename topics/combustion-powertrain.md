# Combustion & Powertrain

This page summarizes the combustion and powertrain simulation topics I work on and how I connect CFD results to engineering decisions.

## Topics I cover

- SI/CI in-cylinder combustion
- Spray breakup and mixture formation
- Ignition delay and combustion phasing
- Charge motion
- Intake/exhaust internal flow
- Water-jacket / coolant-flow analysis
- EATS mixing and flow distribution
- Alternative-fuel exploration
- DOE and geometry optimization

## How I approach these problems

I try to separate the physical question from the numerical setup: what controls mixing, ignition, heat release, pressure development, thermal loading or flow distribution, and what model fidelity is needed to answer that question credibly?

Validation is central: pressure traces, spray/combustion observables, reference trends and consistency checks are used to judge whether the model is useful before optimizing geometry or operating conditions.

## Selected evidence

- SI/CI in-cylinder CFD covering spray, mixture formation, combustion tuning and pressure-trace validation.
- Charge-motion and geometry optimization using DOE, Design Manager, ALTERNOVA and adjoint methods.
- Internal-flow and coolant-flow studies for intake, exhaust, aftertreatment and thermal-management components.
